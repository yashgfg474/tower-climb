# Roblox Context for Tower Climb (as of 2026-10-07)

## Why this game fits 2026
- **RFY is retention-first:** play-through, low bounce, D1 / D2–7 / D8–28, co-play, spend.
- Short co-op runs with a greedy cash-out decision create **repeat sessions** and **friend invites**.
- Ads can seed players; they do **not** substitute for retention.

## What not to copy blindly
Viral simulation / “brainrot” collectible games — different loop. Tower Climb wins as **action extract**, not idle clone.

## Money (later)
- Passes typically ~70% to creator (confirm live docs).
- Creator Rewards replaced legacy Premium/EBP — re-read Creator Hub before promising payouts.
- Ethical: cosmetics / convenience; never gate first clear.
